Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

This page explains what information this website collects when you visit, and what happens to it. It's written in plain language on purpose — if anything here isn't clear, email riley@rclanglands.com and I'll answer directly.

Who this policy covers

This site is run by Riley Langlands, writing as R.C. Langlands, based in Queensland, Australia. I'm not a company — this is a personal author website.

What information is collected

The only personal information this site collects is what you choose to give it:

  • Newsletter sign-up: if you subscribe using the "Stay in the story" form, your name and email address are collected and stored by MailerLite, the email service I use to send updates.
  • Emailing me directly: if you use a "Contact" link to email me, whatever you include in that email is between you and me, like any other email.

This site does not use cookies for tracking or advertising, does not run analytics that identify individual visitors, and does not sell, rent, or share your information with anyone for marketing purposes.

How it's used

Your name and email are used only to send you the newsletter you signed up for — updates on the Seasons series, new Musings posts, and related news. Nothing more.

Who else sees it

Two services help run this site, and each has its own privacy practices:

  • MailerLite — stores newsletter subscriber details and sends the emails.
  • Cloudflare — hosts this website and routes contact emails; a standard web host for any site.

Your choices

You can unsubscribe from the newsletter at any time using the link at the bottom of every email. If you'd like your details removed sooner, or want to know exactly what's on file, email riley@rclanglands.com and I'll action it.

Children's privacy

While this site is about books written for younger readers, the newsletter sign-up is intended for parents, teachers, and adult readers, not for children to use directly. I don't knowingly collect information from children through this site.

Changes to this policy

If how this site handles information ever changes, this page will be updated and the date above will change with it.

Questions

Email riley@rclanglands.com any time.
